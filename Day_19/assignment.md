Day 19 Assignment - SQL Mixed Revision

Objective

Revise all SQL concepts learned from Day 8 to Day 18 by solving mixed practice questions.

---

Questions

Q1

Display employee names earning more than the average company salary.

Q2

Display employee names and their department names.

Q3

Display department names having employees.

Q4

Display employee names working in the HR department.

Q5

Display employee names whose department exists in the DEPARTMENT table.

Q6

Display employee names in uppercase.

Q7

Display the first 3 characters of employee names.

Q8

Display the current date and current month name.

Q9

Display the square root of 144.

Q10

Display employees earning the maximum salary in their department.

Q11

Display department names and employee count.

Q12

Display departments having an average salary greater than 3000.

Q13

Display employees earning the minimum salary in their department.

Q14

Display all employees and their department names.

Q15

Display all departments and their employees.

---

Concepts Covered

- Aggregate Functions
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN
- RIGHT JOIN
- Subqueries
- EXISTS
- Correlated Subqueries
- String Functions
- Date Functions
- Numeric Functions

---

Learning Outcomes

After completing this assignment, you should be able to:

- Write JOIN queries confidently.
- Use aggregate functions with GROUP BY and HAVING.
- Solve subquery and correlated subquery problems.
- Apply string, date and numeric functions.
- Combine multiple SQL concepts in a single query.

# Day 14 Assignment

Display employees whose department numbers are present in the DEPARTMENT table.

### Q2

### Q3

### Q4

Display department names whose department numbers are not present in the EMPLOYEE table.

### Q5

Display employees whose department exists.

### Q6

Display employees whose department does not exist.

### Q7

Display departments having employees.

### Q8

Display departments having no employees.

### Q9

Display employees belonging to departments 10, 20, and 30.

### Q10

Display employees not belonging to departments 10, 20, and 30.

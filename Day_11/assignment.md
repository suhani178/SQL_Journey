# Day 11 — assignment.md

## SQL UPDATE Statement

### Part A — Basic UPDATE

1. Update the salary of employee 101 to 4000.
2. Change the name of employee 102 to 'RAHUL'.
3. Update the department number of employee 103 to 20.
4. Set the salary of employee named 'SMITH' to 3500.
5. Change the job of employee 104 to 'MANAGER'.

### Part B — Conditional UPDATE

6. Increase the salary of employees in department 10 by 500.
7. Set the salary to 2500 for employees earning less than 2000.
8. Increase the salary of employees earning between 2000 and 3000 by 10%.
9. Change the job to 'CLERK' for employees earning less than 1500.
10. Increase the salary by 1000 for employees whose department number is 20.

### Part C — Multiple Columns

11. Update the salary to 4000 and department number to 30 for employee 105.
12. Change the job to 'ANALYST' and salary to 5000 for employee 106.
13. Update the department number to 10 and salary to 3000 for employee named 'ALLEN'.

### Part D — Conceptual Questions

14. What is the purpose of the UPDATE statement?
15. What is the purpose of the SET clause?
16. What happens when UPDATE is executed without a WHERE clause?
17. Which SQL category does UPDATE belong to?
18. Explain the difference between INSERT and UPDATE.
19. Can one UPDATE statement modify multiple rows?
20. Why should you verify the WHERE condition before executing UPDATE?

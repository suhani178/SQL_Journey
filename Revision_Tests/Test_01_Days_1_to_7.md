Common Mistakes Learned

AND vs OR Precedence

Wrong:

SELECT *
FROM EMP
WHERE DEPTNO = 10
OR DEPTNO = 20
AND SAL > 2500;

Correct:

SELECT *
FROM EMP
WHERE (DEPTNO = 10 OR DEPTNO = 20)
AND SAL > 2500;

DDL vs DML

DDL:

- CREATE
- ALTER
- DROP
- TRUNCATE

DML:

- INSERT
